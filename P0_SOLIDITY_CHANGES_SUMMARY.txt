================================================================================
SCORING METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

PILLAR 1: ORDER BLOCKS (15 pts)
  Detection: Swing low/high + 2-bar confirmation
  Proximity scoring: distance from entry in ATR multiples
  Confidence: Looks for recent structure within 15 bars

PILLAR 2: FVG (10 pts)
  Detection: 3-bar imbalance (gap between bar 1 and bar 2)
  Validation: Gap must be unmitigated by bar 3
  Proximity scoring: distance from entry in ATR multiples

PILLAR 3: MULTI-TF CASCADE (10 pts)
  Timeframes: 1H (current), 4H (resampled), Daily (resampled)
  Condition: EMA8 >= EMA21 >= EMA50 (with 0.2% tolerance)
  Scoring: +10 if all 3 agree, +7 if 2/3, +3 if 1/3

PILLAR 4: RISK:REWARD (20 pts max)
  Base scoring: R:R ratio (15pts ≥2.0, 12pts ≥1.5, 8pts ≥1.0)
  Bonus: Stop precision (5pts <1%, 3pts <1.5% from entry)
  Cap: 20 points total (base + bonus cannot exceed 20)
