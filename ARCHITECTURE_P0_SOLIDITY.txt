================================================================================
SCORING LADDER
================================================================================

ORDER BLOCKS (P0-1)
  15 pts → Entry within 0.5 × ATR of order block
  10 pts → Entry within 1.0 × ATR of order block
   5 pts → Entry within 1.5 × ATR of order block
   0 pts → Entry beyond 1.5 × ATR OR no block detected

FVG (P0-2)
  10 pts → Fresh FVG within 1.0 × ATR of entry
   5 pts → Fresh FVG within 2.0 × ATR of entry
   0 pts → No FVG OR beyond 2.0 × ATR

MULTI-TF CASCADE (P0-3)
  10 pts → 1H + 4H + Daily all agree on direction
   7 pts → 2 of 3 timeframes agree on direction
   3 pts → 1 of 3 timeframe agrees on direction
   0 pts → No timeframe agreement OR insufficient data

RISK:REWARD (P0-4)
  15 pts → Base score for R:R ≥ 2.0
  12 pts → Base score for R:R ≥ 1.5
   8 pts → Base score for R:R ≥ 1.0
   0 pts → Base score for R:R < 1.0
  +5 pts → Bonus if stop < 1.0% from entry
  +3 pts → Bonus if stop < 1.5% from entry
  MAX:20 → Base + bonus capped at 20 points

TOTAL: 0-55 points (55 = perfect structural confidence)
