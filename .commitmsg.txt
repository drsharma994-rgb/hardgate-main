hg-v1308: repair two modules that index.html loads but that cannot parse

Found while checking whether the 49 red suites shared one root cause. They do not -
every one I sampled is red on origin/main independently - but the sweep DID find two
modules that index.html loads and that cannot parse at all. All 229 standalone
top-level modules parse now; two did not before.

1) gold-scalp.js (HG_GoldScalpEngine) HAD NEVER PARSED. Traced through the file's own
   history: 7 revisions, and the last good one is dd7a40ad (155 lines, balanced).
   The next commit, 94350044 "Complete Phase 1-4 implementation", collapsed a block
   onto a single line, dropped a closing brace, and in doing so also left a
   setup.direction test against "long" on a setup whose direction is BEAR, plus a
   reference to an adr that is not in scope. I verified the imbalance by bisecting the
   file (line 142 parses when closed, 143 does not) and by testing candidate tails -
   every tail failed, which is what proved the damage was inside the body and not at
   the end.
   WHY IT MATTERED: gold-core-engine.js instantiates this engine for the gold JUDAS
   read - new root.HG_GoldScalpEngine().evaluateScalp(rows, rows, macro, silver). The
   file never loaded, so that read has silently returned null inside its try/catch
   since 94350044, and no test covered it. Restored from dd7a40ad keeping the one
   legitimate intent from the breaking commit - the ATR stop buffer 0.40 -> 0.50.

2) ganesh-gold.js was corrupted by a botched shell append: a stray redirect artifact
   was pasted into the source, an orphaned duplicate of tmCrownedCardHTML's BODY
   followed with its function line consumed (so it could not parse), a dead stub
   returning the string "test" sat above the real function, and the IIFE was missing
   its closer. Repaired to one function, one export, balanced braces.

Neither repair turns a red suite green - I checked test-goldscalp, test-golddirection,
test-gold-census, test-all-tabs-mount and test-candle-forecast before and after and
all five are still red for upstream's own reasons. These are latent-defect repairs,
shipped because a gold engine that cannot load is worth fixing regardless.
