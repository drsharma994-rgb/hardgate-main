THE THREE P3 INDICATORS
================================================================================

P3.1: ORDER FLOW CONFLUENCE (12 points)
---------------------------------------
Function: window.hgOmniOrderFlowScore(setup)

What it does:
  Scores entry alignment with order flow imbalance (bid/ask bias).
  Detects when your direction matches strong order flow support.

Scoring:
  12 pts: +2σ imbalance aligned (strong conviction)
  10 pts: +1σ imbalance aligned (moderate)
   5 pts: ±0.5σ imbalance aligned (light)
   0 pts: Counterflow or order flow unavailable

Bonus: +3 pts if sustained >5 bars (capped at 12)

Why it matters:
  Order flow is a true edge. When flow aligns with directional bias,
  it's confirmation you're on the right side.


P3.2: STRUCTURAL SUPPORT/RESISTANCE CONFLUENCE (15 points)
----------------------------------------------------------
Function: window.hgOmniStructureConfluenceScore(setup)

What it does:
  Scores entry proximity to multi-touch structural levels.
  Detects confluence of swing highs/lows, round numbers, VWAP.

Scoring:
  15 pts: Entry within 0.25x ATR of 2+ structural levels
  10 pts: Entry within 0.25x ATR of 1 level, OR 0.5x ATR of 2+ levels
   5 pts: Entry within 0.5x ATR of 1 level, OR 1.0x ATR of 2+ levels
   0 pts: No structural proximity

Bonus: +3 pts if stop on structural level (capped at 15)

Why it matters:
  Wyckoff-level thinking. Entry within 0.25x ATR of 2+ levels =
  high-probability entry zone. Stop on structure = disciplined risk.


P3.3: MOMENTUM CONVERGENCE (12 points)
--------------------------------------
Function: window.hgOmniMomentumConvergenceScore(setup)

What it does:
  Scores alignment of 3 momentum indicators: RSI, MACD, Stochastic.
  Confirmation layer: when technicals all agree, momentum is strong.

Scoring:
  12 pts: All 3 agree on direction
   8 pts: 2 of 3 agree
   4 pts: 1 of 3 aligns
   0 pts: None available or all disagree

Indicators:
  RSI(14): >50 bullish, <50 bearish
  MACD(12,26,9): >0 bullish, <0 bearish
  Stochastic(14): >50 bullish, <50 bearish

Why it matters:
  When RSI, MACD, and Stochastic all point the same direction,
  momentum confirmation is strong. Technical traders live by this.
