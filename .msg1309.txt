hg-v1309: CANDLE FORECAST — the engine its guard documents now exists, and the tab renders

Three fixes, all on the tab the user asked about. Suite: 44 failed -> 40.

1) goldind.js goldCandleForecast REBUILT to the spec tests/test-candle-forecast.mjs
   already documented but which had never been implemented:
     - direction: a SHRUNK DRIFT with |t| >= 1 replacing the one-bar EMA9 tick
       (`ema9[n-1] > ema9[n-2]`), which flipped on noise and always named a side.
     - band: the EMPIRICAL 60th percentile of the last 100 true ranges replacing
       `atr * 0.8`, an arbitrary multiple with no estimator behind it.
     - volatility: Parkinson high-low variance blended with ATR through a bounded
       EWMA (lambda 0.94), reported in price units as `vol`.
     - `strength`: bounded |t| in [0,4], replacing the `confidence` field, which
       was atr/price — the volatility ratio, about 0.002 on gold — printed on the
       card as a "Confidence Score" that read 0% on every bar.
     - a 30-bar floor (was 20), and an honest NEUTRAL third state that carries NO
       levels: `sign` defaulted to -1, so a NEUTRAL call would otherwise have
       emitted a stop and two targets for a side the desk had just declined.

   THE REAL DEFECT FOUND WHILE DOING IT: a TIE OF ZEROES WAS BEING BROKEN AS IF IT
   WERE A TIE. Every read abstains on a dead-flat tape, so bulls === bears === 0,
   and the tie-break handed the tape to whichever side `closes[n-1] >= closes[n-2]`
   picked — on an exactly flat tape that is BULL, because 100 >= 100. A side
   invented from nothing, with a stop and two targets attached.

   Two states are now separate at the top level rather than conflated: `null` means
   the tape could not be measured (thin, junk, or no range at all), while NEUTRAL
   means a readable tape whose drift sits inside the noise band. The previous
   no-range branch returned a partial object carrying dir:null, which forced the
   caller to distinguish "read it and found no side" from "could not read it" by
   inspecting a nested field.

2) gold-candle-forecast.js: THE TAB RENDERED NOTHING ON MOUNT. Its double-mount

   EVERY selector truthy, so any such guard fires on the FIRST mount. Guarded on
