hg-v1311: CANDLE FORECAST gains an advanced pattern layer (disclosure, not weight)

The user asked for more advanced candlestick recognition on the CANDLE FORECAST tab
for more accurate gold setups. This adds it without falling into the trap that makes
"more patterns" actively worse.

THE TRAP I HAD TO AVOID FIRST. `hgCfCandlePatterns` VOTES. Its families add to
`bulls`/`bears`, which decide the direction call, and its stops become the trade's own
stop (`rawStop = dir === 'BULL' ? pat.longStop : pat.shortStop`). So adding voting
families would have silently doubled the pattern weight on a live trade decision —
the hg-v966 unmeasured-weight trap — and, since three-line strike and three-outside
both begin with the same two bars the harami/star logic already reads, it could
re-count one event as two (hg-v1153's own warning).

So the new layer is DISCLOSURE. Every pattern is recorded and rides the result as
`advancedPatterns` / `patternLeans` / `patternWhere` / `patternDrift`. Nothing in it
gates, scores or prices anything, and the guard asserts both textually and
behaviourally that no advanced value reaches `bulls`, `bears`, `checked` or `rawStop`.
The forward ledger decides out of sample whether any of it separates winners.

WHAT IT ADDS

  Four-bar reversals: bullish/bearish three-line strike, three outside up/down, and
  abandoned baby — the only member of the star family that requires a real gap on
  both sides of the doji.

  Continuations: rising and falling three methods, tasuki gap (the unfilled gap is
  the whole point, so the code checks the close stays above the gap bar's low),
  side-by-side white lines.

  Exhaustion and structure: three-bar climax (three expanding ranges with shrinking
  bodies — participation rising into a stall), double inside (compression) and double
  outside (expansion).

  THE ACCURACY HALF — location and drift context. A hammer at a swing low is a
  reversal; the same hammer at a swing high is a trap, and the three-bar voter above
  cannot tell them apart. Every pattern now carries where it sat in the recent
  20-bar range and whether the drift there runs with or against it, three-state
  throughout (`at the range high` / `at the range low` / `mid-range`; `up` / `down`
  / `flat`). A flat window reads flat rather than being guessed as up or down.

FOUND WHILE BUILDING IT: the layer read `.length` before validating its input, so a
null tape threw instead of failing open. Fixed — the array check now precedes the
length read, which was exactly the throw it existed to prevent.

TEST DISCIPLINE: building this guard took four wrong fixtures in a row, and every one
of them was MY error, not the detector's — bars placed in the wrong index slots (the
detector reads a=rows[n-4] … d=rows[n-1], so a three-bar pattern needs a lead-in),
an inside bar that poked outside its container, and "small" bars below the body
floor. Each was found by measuring the lifted function rather than re-reading the
condition, and the comments record the slot mapping so the next pack does not repeat
it.

Tests: tests/test-candle-forecast-advanced.mjs — 26 assertions over 6 sections,
including the non-voting invariant and thin/junk/degenerate tape handling.

BATCH: hg-v1310 -> hg-v1311 across all five surfaces, recorded in the ledger
(15 entries). The discipline guard is green at 90 assertions.
