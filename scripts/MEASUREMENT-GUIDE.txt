THE MEASUREMENT
───────────────
For every OMNIROUTE detection (or simulated order):
  1. Record entry price, market price, calculated stop
  2. Calculate gap in R: |entry - market| / risk
  3. Walk forward N bars: did price ever trade the entry?
  4. Bucket by gap: [0–0.25R], [0.25–0.5R], [0.5–1R], [1R+]
  5. Report: % never-filled per bucket for 4h and 1h horizons
