hg-v1313: CANDLE FORECAST gains the gaps and windows family

The user asked for more advanced candlestick recognition on CANDLE FORECAST, with
other pattern recognition that helps SETUP FORMATION, and a version bump. Version is
hg-v1312 -> hg-v1313 across all five surfaces, recorded in data/batch-ledger.json.

THE BLIND SPOT THIS CLOSES. The file now carries forty-odd classic shapes, and NOT
ONE of them read a gap as a gap. Every voter compares bodies, wicks and containment —
none of them compares two bars' RANGES — so a window, which is precisely a bar whose
whole range sits clear of the previous bar's range, was invisible unless it happened
to form an engulfing or a tasuki pattern on top of it.

WHY THIS IS THE SETUP-FORMATION HALF, not just more names: a gap is classified by the
size of the run-up that PRECEDED it, measured in ATRs so it reads the same in any
volatility regime —

    run-up under 1.5 ATR  -> BREAKAWAY   a move is starting; the gap opens the range
    run-up to 4 ATR       -> RUNAWAY     a move is running; continuation
    run-up beyond 4 ATR   -> EXHAUSTION the gap is the late gasp, not a fresh entry

and an exhaustion gap is additionally named as such. The STAGE is what tells a reader
whether to enter or stand down, which is why the gap is classified rather than merely
labelled. An unmeasurable run-up leaves the stage unread rather than guessed.

ALSO ADDED
  - ISLAND REVERSAL (both directions): gapped in, held clear of the range on both
    sides, gapped back out. The strongest structure in the gap family and previously
    undetectable by anything in the file.
  - THREE-BAR REVERSAL: the middle bar takes out the first bar's extreme and closes
    back inside it, then a bar closes beyond the other extreme — a failed breakout
    plus a confirmation, which nothing here tested.
  - LADDER BOTTOM / TOP: a run of declining (or rising) closes, then a strong bar
    opening beyond the last of them.

TWO REAL DEFECTS FOUND AND FIXED WHILE MEASURING THE NEW CODE
  1. The gap check fired on an EARLIER bar's window, so a stale gap was reported as a
     fresh one on the last bar. It now reads only the latest bar's window.
  2. The two island directions were chained with `else`, which silently suppressed the
     bearish island whenever the bullish test also evaluated true. They are now
     independent tests — neither is the other's fallback.

DISCLOSURE, NOT WEIGHT — the invariant of this whole layer, unchanged. Nothing added
here votes, and the guard asserts the gap stage never reaches bulls, bears, checked or
rawStop. hgCfCandlePatterns still owns the vote; this layer is recorded and the forward
ledger decides out of sample whether any of it separates winners.

Tests: tests/test-candle-forecast-advanced.mjs, 35 assertions over 7 sections, with the
new section 4b covering every pattern above, the breakaway/exhaustion split on the
SAME window shape, and the non-voting assertion. Twelve affected CANDLE FORECAST and
gold suites run green.
